The problem

Hardware supply for MSPs and IT providers, built for fast scanning by technical buyers.

Griffin IT's unique selling proposition, stock your shelf for free, pay after you sell, was buried in a cluttered old website. They needed a clean, direct site putting this zero-risk model front and centre for IT resellers and MSPs.

What I built

Griffin IT in 7 parts.

  1. 01Complete WordPress and Elementor rebuild with the unique stock model as the hero message
  2. 02Clear above-the-fold value proposition: stock, sell, then pay
  3. 03Service and product category pages for MSP hardware solutions
  4. 04Designed for a B2B audience: IT resellers and managed service providers
  5. 05Lead capture forms for partnership and wholesale enquiries
  6. 06Performance optimisation across the WordPress installation
  7. 07Plugin audit and conflict resolution
In short

The pay-after-you-sell model is genuinely disruptive in IT hardware supply. That message was lost on the old site. I rebuilt Griffin IT with that headline front and centre, supporting pages explaining the process in simple terms for a busy IT reseller.
