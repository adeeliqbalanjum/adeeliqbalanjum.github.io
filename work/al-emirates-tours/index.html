The problem

Every tour page was being built by hand. One template and a tour post type, with a live price booking form.

Al Emirates Tours is a UAE tour operator competing in a crowded Dubai tourism market. They needed a professional website showcasing their desert, city, and adventure tours while capturing direct bookings before visitors go to comparison aggregators.

What I built

Al Emirates Tours in 7 parts.

  1. 01WordPress and Elementor Pro build with UAE-inspired warm premium design
  2. 02Tour listing pages: desert safari, city tours, adventure packages, and transfers
  3. 03Individual tour pages with itinerary, pricing, inclusions, and exclusions
  4. 04WhatsApp integration for direct enquiries, critical for the UAE market
  5. 05Enquiry and booking forms for the UAE mobile-dominant audience
  6. 06SEO structure targeting Dubai tourism keywords
  7. 07Mobile-first build throughout
In short

In the UAE tourism market, WhatsApp is as important as a booking form, customers expect to message instantly. I built Al Emirates Tours with WhatsApp CTAs on every tour page alongside traditional forms, giving two conversion channels.
