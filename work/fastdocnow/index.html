The problem

Nervous patients needed to trust a telehealth brand fast. Clear service pages and a consultation flow.

FastDocNow needed a conversion-focused telehealth website that clearly communicates online consultation and prescription services to US patients. Healthcare visitors are cautious, the site had to build trust fast while driving them toward booking an appointment.

What I built

FastDocNow in 7 parts.

  1. 01Full Elementor Pro build with healthcare-specific layouts and trust signals throughout
  2. 02Dedicated service pages for consultations, prescription refills, chronic disease management, and imaging
  3. 03Clear booking flow with prominent CTAs above the fold on every page
  4. 04Trust builders: licensed provider profiles and patient testimonials
  5. 05WP Rocket performance optimisation
  6. 06Mobile-first build since majority of telehealth traffic comes from smartphones
  7. 07Blog section for SEO targeting healthcare search terms
In short

Healthcare sites face a unique challenge: the visitor is already stressed and needs quick answers. Every unclear CTA and every slow page loses a potential patient. I structured FastDocNow with a clear hierarchy, what you get, who the providers are, how to book, with performance tuning throughout.
