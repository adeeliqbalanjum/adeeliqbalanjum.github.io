The problem

Smart home and AV for homes, businesses and government, organised so each buyer finds their own route.

Artisan Technologies provides residential and commercial smart-home automation but their old site failed to convey the premium nature of their installations. They needed a site that felt as polished as their physical product, with clear residential vs commercial splits and a tutorial resource hub.

What I built

Artisan Technologies in 7 parts.

  1. 01Full WordPress and Elementor Pro rebuild from scratch with a clean, modern design
  2. 02Split architecture: Residential and Commercial sections each with their own use-case sub-pages
  3. 03Gallery section to showcase completed smart-home installations
  4. 04Brands page listing compatible home automation hardware
  5. 05Tutorial hub to increase time-on-site and establish expertise
  6. 06Blog for content marketing targeting smart-home keywords
  7. 07Mobile-responsive with clear contact CTAs across all service pages
In short

Smart home companies sell a feeling as much as a product. The Artisan Technologies site needed to match that premium aesthetic. I structured it so a residential homeowner and a commercial property manager can each immediately find their section without the other's content getting in the way.
