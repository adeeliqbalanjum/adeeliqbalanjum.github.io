The problem

A veteran owned junk removal business with a quote route on every screen.

Junk Veteran is a veteran-owned junk removal company in Massachusetts. They needed a local-SEO-focused site that converts quickly, junk removal is an urgent same-day need. The site had to rank for local search terms and get the phone ringing with minimal friction.

What I built

Junk Veteran in 7 parts.

  1. 01WordPress and Elementor Pro build with local SEO architecture from the start
  2. 02Service pages for each junk type: furniture, appliances, construction debris, estate cleanouts
  3. 03Location pages targeting Massachusetts cities for local SEO
  4. 04Prominent phone number and same-day CTA above the fold on every page
  5. 05Veteran-owned trust messaging throughout
  6. 06Before and after project gallery to demonstrate quality
  7. 07Google Business Profile integration and review display
In short

Junk removal is hyper-local and often urgent. Someone needing their garage cleared is not going to read five paragraphs, they need a phone number in three seconds or they call a competitor. I built Junk Veteran with the phone number and service area as the first things a visitor sees.
