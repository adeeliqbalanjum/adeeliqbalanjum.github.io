The problem

Reusable moving crate rental in Denver, explained simply enough to order from a phone.

Relocrate offers reusable plastic moving crates as a sustainable alternative to cardboard boxes in Denver. The concept is innovative but unfamiliar, most people default to buying cardboard. The website needed to quickly explain the concept, overcome objections, and convert visitors to a crate rental booking.

What I built

Relocrate in 7 parts.

  1. 01WordPress and Elementor Pro build with a fresh, eco-focused brand aesthetic
  2. 02How it works section explaining the delivery, move, and collection process in three simple steps
  3. 03Comparison section: Relocrate vs cardboard boxes, cost, time, environmental impact
  4. 04Residential and commercial moving service pages
  5. 05Online booking or enquiry form with service area map for Denver coverage
  6. 06Environmental impact section to appeal to sustainability-conscious movers
  7. 07Pricing page with clear crate count calculator for different home sizes
In short

Relocrate is solving a genuine problem, cardboard boxes are expensive, wasteful, and annoying, but the solution is unfamiliar. I structured it with how-it-works as the primary message, followed immediately by the cardboard comparison to remove the main objection, then a clear booking CTA.
