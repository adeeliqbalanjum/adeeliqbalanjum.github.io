The problem

A speaker and podcast host who needed one home for talks, episodes and enquiries.

Todd Malloy is a speaker, podcast host, and philosopher behind a personal growth framework called Toddism. His brand needed a website that communicated the depth of his philosophy while making it easy for listeners, event organisers, and followers to engage.

What I built

Todd Malloy in 7 parts.

  1. 01WordPress and Elementor Pro personal brand build centred on Todd's philosophy and content
  2. 02Toddism philosophy page explaining the core principles in an engaging visual layout
  3. 03Podcast showcase section with episode listings and guest appearance archive
  4. 04Speaker and podcast guest booking section for event organisers
  5. 05Blog and content hub for ongoing philosophy content
  6. 06Social proof section with media appearances
  7. 07Email opt-in integration for audience list building
In short

Personal brand sites for speakers need to convey personality and depth while still being functional. For Todd, the site needed to make you feel his philosophy before you read a single word, then give you clear paths whether you are a fan, a podcast producer, or an event organiser.
