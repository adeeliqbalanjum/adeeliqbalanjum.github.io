The problem

A financial adviser site where a calm first impression and an easy way to book a call matter most.

Seva Wealth is a financial advisory firm whose name comes from the Sanskrit concept of compassionate service. Their website needed to communicate that philosophy alongside serious financial credentials, attracting clients who want a values-driven advisor.

What I built

Seva Wealth in 7 parts.

  1. 01WordPress and Elementor Pro build with a sophisticated, trust-heavy financial services design
  2. 02About and philosophy page communicating the Seva values and advisor background
  3. 03Service pages covering wealth management, financial planning, and investment advisory
  4. 04Team and credentials section for regulatory trust, critical in financial services
  5. 05Calendly integration for direct meeting scheduling from every CTA
  6. 06Secure financial planning portal link integration for existing clients
  7. 07Compliance-conscious copy and design meeting financial services standards
In short

Financial services websites have a specific credibility challenge, visitors are about to trust someone with their life savings. For Seva Wealth, the values-driven philosophy needed to feel genuine rather than marketed. I built the site so the Seva story leads and the financial credentials follow.
