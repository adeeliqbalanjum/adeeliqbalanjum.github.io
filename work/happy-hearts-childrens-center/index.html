The problem

Parents choosing a daycare want reassurance and a tour booking, in that order.

Happy Hearts Children's Center is a Herndon-based daycare competing against national chains. Parents researching childcare are making one of the most important decisions of their lives, the site needed to be warm, trustworthy, and immediately answer the questions every parent has about safety, curriculum, and enrollment.

What I built

Happy Hearts Children's Center in 7 parts.

  1. 01WordPress and WP Rocket build with performance-critical configuration for image-heavy pages
  2. 02Warm, friendly design that matches the brand, approachable without being childish
  3. 03Program pages: infant care, toddler, preschool, each with age-appropriate curriculum detail
  4. 04Tour scheduling CTA prominent on every page
  5. 05Parent-facing FAQ covering safety, licensing, staff qualifications, and meals
  6. 06Photo gallery of the facility and classrooms
  7. 07WP Rocket configuration for image-heavy pages
In short

Parents choosing childcare are reading between the lines of every website element. Happy Hearts answers all of those questions before the parent consciously asks them, through warm imagery, clear staff and licensing information, and an easy tour CTA.
