The problem

Destinations and services laid out so a holiday enquiry takes one form.

Book My Holidays UK needed a professional holiday booking website targeting UK travellers, with clear destination listings, service pages for different holiday types, and a smooth enquiry-to-booking flow.

What I built

Book My Holidays UK in 7 parts.

  1. 01WordPress and Elementor Pro build with a polished, trust-led UK travel aesthetic
  2. 02Destination pages covering major holiday regions
  3. 03Service sections: package holidays, cruises, city breaks, and tailor-made travel
  4. 04About Us and trust sections to differentiate from low-credibility competitors
  5. 05Holiday enquiry forms with clear response time commitments
  6. 06Blog section for destination guides and SEO
  7. 07Mobile-responsive throughout
In short

UK travel consumers are among the most review-conscious in the world. Book My Holidays UK needed to look credible immediately, I focused on trust signals alongside clear destination and package pages that make the enquiry process feel effortless.
