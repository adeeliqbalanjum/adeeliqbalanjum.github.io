The problem

A Dallas Fort Worth roofer who needed local search visibility and quote requests.

Hercules Roof System is a roofing contractor serving Frisco and the wider DFW area. In a highly competitive local services market, their website needed to be found by local searchers, immediately convey trustworthiness, and convert visitors to quote requests.

What I built

Hercules Roof System in 7 parts.

  1. 01WordPress and Elementor Pro build with a strong, professional roofing industry aesthetic
  2. 02Residential and commercial roofing service pages
  3. 03Local SEO structure targeting Frisco, Plano, McKinney, and surrounding DFW suburbs
  4. 04Prominent free quote CTA with phone number above the fold on every page
  5. 05Insurance claim assistance page, a major conversion driver for storm damage roofing
  6. 06Project gallery and before-after section
  7. 07Google reviews integration and trust badge section
In short

Roofing sites in Texas compete on credibility after a hailstorm. Hercules needed a site that looked more established and trustworthy than the competition, with local SEO targeting the specific DFW zip codes they serve.
