The problem

HR outsourcing for small businesses, restructured around services, trust and one enquiry route.

Griffin Resources provides HR outsourcing, payroll, recruiting, and marketing services to small businesses. Their site needed to clearly communicate a wide service offering while making time-poor business owners quickly understand the value and convert to a consultation.

What I built

Griffin Resources in 7 parts.

  1. 01WordPress and Elementor Pro build with a professional, corporate-but-approachable design
  2. 02Service architecture covering payroll, recruiting, coaching, and marketing
  3. 03Lead-generation focused layout with consultation CTAs on every page
  4. 04Trust section with testimonials and business metrics
  5. 05Mobile-responsive design targeting busy business owners on the go
  6. 06SEO friendly structure for HR outsourcing keywords
  7. 07CRM form integration for lead capture
In short

HR services sites are inherently complex, payroll, compliance, recruiting, and coaching each deserve their own explanation. I built Griffin Resources so a small business owner can identify their pain point in under 10 seconds and reach a consultation CTA without reading walls of text.
