How a fix works

The same four steps on every troubleshooting order.

1

Diagnose

You send the URL, what changed before it broke, and the error or screenshot. I reproduce it and trace the cause.

2

Backup and staging

Full backup before anything is touched. Fix on a staging copy where the hosting allows it.

3

Fix and test

Fix applied, then checkout or the affected pages tested end to end on desktop and mobile, with a test order where relevant.

4

Report

A short written note: what was wrong, what changed, what was tested, and anything else worth fixing next.
